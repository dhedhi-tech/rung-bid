# Patta Party: Summary

## The game
- Online team bidding card game (Rung / Court Piece style) for 4, 6 or 8 players, two teams.
- Website: www.pattaparty.com. Android app (Play Store closed test, developer: Dhedhi Tech).
- Code: github.com/dhedhi-tech/rung-bid (Render hosting, Supabase accounts). Project notes: CLAUDE.md in the repo.
- Features: play with friends via WhatsApp invite links, practice with smart bots, open tables, voice and text chat, emoji and stickers, Google sign-in, levels, XP, coins, table themes, leaderboard, friends, Joker mode (our USP), guided first game with a coach (new players usually win their first hand), mute and report players, admin page with stats.

## Marketing motive
- Goal: build a "line" of players before launch, not after.
- Audience: desi friends and families who love Rung (Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, overseas).
- Their pain (from rival app reviews): too many ads, fake bot "online" players, no private tables with friends, bad bots, wrong house rules, crashes.
- Our edge: play with your own people with voice chat, no ads during play, 6 and 8 player tables, bidding Rung and Joker mode, guided first game.
- Emotional hook: family Rung nostalgia.
- Rival to watch: rung.gg (4 players only, similar features).
