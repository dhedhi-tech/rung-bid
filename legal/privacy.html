Privacy Policy

Rung Bid is an online team card game. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. We use the name you type and a random player ID stored in your browser so you can rejoin your seat. This stays on our game server only while your table is open.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. We use them only to create your Rung Bid profile and show your name and photo at the table. We store your game progress: level, XP, coins, games played, wins, bids and table themes you own.

Chat, voice and speech typing

  • Table chat: the last 50 messages of a table are kept in the server's memory while the table is open, so late joiners can read them. They are deleted when the table closes. We do not save chat history.
  • Voice chat: audio goes directly between players' devices, or through a relay server when a direct connection is not possible. We do not record or store voice.
  • Speech typing: your browser (for example Google Chrome or Apple Safari) turns your speech into text. Rung Bid only receives the text you choose to send.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, show you personal ads based on it, or share it with anyone except the services that run the game: our hosting provider (Render), our database and sign-in provider (Supabase) and Google sign-in.

Deleting your data

To delete your profile and progress, contact us at {{CONTACT}} from the email you signed in with. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Rung Bid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below.
