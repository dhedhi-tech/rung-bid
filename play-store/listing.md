## English (default)

### App name (30 max)
Patta Party: Rung Court Piece

### Short description (80 max)
Rung & Court Piece online with friends. Bidding card game for 4, 6 or 8 players

### Full description
Patta Party is Rung, the team bidding card game you grew up playing, now online. Also called Court Piece or Rang. Sit with your partner, bid how many tricks your team can win, call trump and play it out.

PLAY WITH YOUR OWN PEOPLE
• Send a table link on WhatsApp and your friends join in one tap
• 4, 6 or 8 players in two teams
• Voice chat with your table, plus text chat, emoji reactions and stickers
• Family in Dubai, cousins in Karachi, friends in Lahore: one table

JOKER MODE
One Joker per deck can flip a trick to the other team. Play it at the right moment and steal the hand.

PRACTICE ANYTIME
• Smart bots fill empty seats and back up your play
• Join open tables and play with new people
• New to Rung? A coach guides you through your first hand, step by step

NO ADS DURING PLAY
Your game is never interrupted by ads.

LEVEL UP
• Earn XP and coins every hand
• Unlock table themes and badges
• Climb the leaderboard
• Add friends and invite them to your table

Quick, Normal or Full games. Play as a guest or sign in with Google to keep your progress.

If you like trump card games such as Call Break or Spades, try Rung with bidding and the Joker twist.
