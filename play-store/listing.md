## Full description
Patta Party is the team bidding card game you grew up playing, now online. Sit with your partner, bid how many tricks your team can win, call trump and play it out.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 4, 6 or 8 players in two teams
• Play with friends using a table link on WhatsApp
• Practice anytime with smart bots
• Join open tables and play with new people

JOKER MODE
One Joker per deck can flip a trick to the other team. Play it at the right moment and steal the hand.

LEARN IN MINUTES
New to the game? A coach guides you through your first hand, step by step.

TALK WHILE YOU PLAY
• Voice chat with your table
• Text chat, emoji reactions and stickers

LEVEL UP
• Earn XP and coins every hand
• Unlock table themes and badges
• Climb the leaderboard
• Add friends and invite them to your table

Quick, Normal or Full games. Play as a guest or sign in with Google to keep your progress.
